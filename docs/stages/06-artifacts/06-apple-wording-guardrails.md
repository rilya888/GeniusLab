# Этап 06 — Apple Wording Guardrails

## Allowed direction

- Neutral service descriptions.
- Clear statement of independent repair service.
- No implied affiliation unless legally supported.

## Avoid patterns

- Claims that can imply official authorization without proof.
- Absolute and unverifiable guarantees.
- Ambiguous use of manufacturer warranty language.

## Recommended disclaimer pattern

"Genius Lab è un laboratorio indipendente e non affiliato ad Apple Inc., salvo diversa esplicita indicazione legale."

(Финальная формулировка подтверждается legal-owner.)
